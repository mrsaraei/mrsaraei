<p align="justify">I am a Ph.D. scholar in Biomedical Engineering at Telkes Neuroengineering Lab, specializing in advanced multimodal physiological signal analysis, including EEG, ECG, EMG, wearable biosensors (ActiGraph), and fNIRS. My work combines neuroelectrophysiology, real-time signal processing, and machine learning to support next-generation neuromodulation research. I focus on characterizing spinal–brain neural dynamics across pre- and post-operative phases, emphasizing frequency patterns, pulse–train structure, electrode interactions, and large-scale neural connectivity. I develop and implement robust biosignal processing and machine learning pipelines in MATLAB and Python to identify biomarkers that can guide personalized spinal cord stimulation therapies for patients with chronic pain and motor control disorders. I aim to advance into a Lead Biomedical Scientist role, where I can leverage my technical expertise, translational research experience, and interdisciplinary problem-solving skills to drive clinical innovation in neurotechnology and patient-specific neuromodulation.</p>
